The world is a big place

This is a one-person project: one rider, every street on Earth. It improves every week — time trials, weather, landmarks, snow above the treeline, and whatever breaks when someone rides somewhere nobody has ridden before.

If you enjoy it, support keeps the map servers running and the updates coming.
